💡 Developer Insights
The industry is undergoing a massive strategic change: resources are rapidly shifting from traditional talent pools to heavy investment in AI infrastructure and application development. This requires every developer to confront how AI tools are automating workflows and redefining our role.

Competing with the foundational models built by tech giants is simply unsustainable for most companies. YCO has strategically evolved in response. Instead of relying solely on models built by our internal AI team, we now strategically leverage the most powerful and known external models across image, video, and audio generation, and deliver them to our users as fast as possible.

This agile change sharpens our focus. The core question is no longer "How do we generate?" but: "What's next after users have created an incredible piece of work?"

For us at Perfect, the answer lies in the application layer. Delivering delicate, specialized, and unique adjustments that foundational models cannot achieve alone. For example, specialized refinement in Makeup, Face, Skin, Body, and Beauty effects is the unique value we bring to the market.
